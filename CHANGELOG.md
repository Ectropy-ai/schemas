# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- **`_definitions/urn` 1.0.0 is served again, unchanged.** The versions published before 2.0.0 refer to it at its own address, so a schema pinned at one of them resolves again. It is superseded: new records use `_definitions/record-id`, and no current schema refers to it.

## 2.0.0

- **Record ids are issuer-owned https URIs (breaking).** `_definitions/record-id` 1.0.0 replaces `_definitions/urn`: a record's id, and every id it points at, is an https URI on a domain its issuer controls, with no query or fragment -- the form GS1 CBV 2.0 section 8.4.4 uses for a location and EPCIS 2.0 for any object without a GS1 key. Anyone can issue records under their own domain; no issuer is named by the standard. To move a 1.x id, keep its issuer, type and id and write them as a path under the issuer's domain: `urn:{namespace}:{issuer}:{type}:{id}` becomes `https://{issuer domain}/{issuer}/{type}/{id}`, any further colon-separated segments as further path segments. Every schema that took a URN takes a record id: `pm/consequence`, `pm/decision`, `pm/inspection`, `pm/participant` and `pm/schedule-proposal` 3.0.0, `resource/resource-pose` and `resource/resource-status` 3.0.0, `voxel/voxel-group` 3.0.0, `voxel/voxel-v3` 4.0.0, `_definitions/configuration-ref` and `_definitions/provenance` 0.2.0, `flow/vendor-price-submission` 0.5.0. `flow/cost-rollup` 0.2.0 changes a description only.
- **`flow/flow-common` 0.7.0 (breaking).** `projectUri`, `tenantUri` and `workerRef` are issued under any issuer's own domain -- `https://{issuer domain}[/{path}]/project/{UUID}`, `.../tenant/{id}`, `.../worker/{pseudonym}` -- as `locationUri` and `partyUri` already are; a `unitId` that is not a GS1 EPC URN is any https URI under its issuer's domain.
- **Examples.** Every example id is under `https://example.org/`; the tariff examples describe a fictional utility.

### Additions since the 1.0.0 notes

- **`_definitions/authority` 0.1.2.** The fabrication label set is this standard's own: its seven labels name the levels of this standard's scale for a fabrication shop. Descriptions only; every earlier instance validates unchanged.
- **New: `_definitions/authority` 0.1.0.** One scale of authority for every domain: a `level` from 0 to 6, read with a domain's `labelSet` -- its name for each of the seven levels, with what each means and the published documents it is read from. Three label sets: `generic`; `construction-commercial` (FIELD, FOREMAN, SUPERINTENDENT, PM, ARCHITECT, OWNER, REGULATORY); and `fabrication` (OPERATOR, LEAD, SUPERVISOR, PRODUCTION_MANAGER, ENGINEER, OWNER, REGULATORY), read from ANSI/ISA-95.00.01-2025, ISO 3834-2:2021, ISO 14731:2019, ISO 14732:2025, ISO 10218-2:2025, ISO 9001:2026 and 29 CFR 1910.147 and 1910.252. In fabrication, ENGINEER is the fabricator's own process and quality authority, not the owner's engineer of record. `authority` is a level read in its domain, with the domain's label for it if stated; the schema refuses a label the domain does not give that level. Who holds a level and what it may approve are set by a project or an organisation, not here. Additions only.
- **`pm/decision` 2.4.0, `pm/participant` 2.1.0.** Additions only: `authorityLevel.domain` and `authorityDomain` name the label set a level is read with; absent, it is read as construction-commercial. Levels are the `_definitions/authority` scale. Every instance valid at 2.3.0 and 2.0.0 stays valid.
- **New: `flow/candidate-site` 0.1.0.** A place a shop could run, as its energy supply sees it: its location (WGS 84, and how the point was found), its country and administrative areas (ISO 3166 and national codes such as FIPS, NUTS or LAU), every utility or distribution system operator with territory there (by identifier, from a cited dataset such as Form EIA-861), the serving utility where known, its grid region, the grid factors and tariffs it is read with, what the grid can take there (hosting capacity, available load capacity, the interconnection queue, each cited), and the substations, lines and plants near it as OpenStreetMap records them -- each with its voltages and distance -- with the query, radius, licence and read time. The gate refuses a serving utility the site's list does not name; the schema refuses dynamic pricing without its market and a contracted demand without its capacity. Additions only.
- **`_definitions/units` 1.5.0.** Additions: VLT (volt, V) and KVT (kilovolt, kV), electric potential; KVA (kilovolt-ampere, kVA), apparent power; KVR (kilovar, kvar), reactive power. Every 1.4.0 instance validates unchanged.
- **New: `flow/utility-tariff` 0.1.0.** An electricity tariff as its utility publishes it, from any country: the utility (by its identifiers under any scheme -- an EIA-861 number, an ENTSO-E EIC, a regulator's registry), the schedule's code, name, sector and service voltage, the dates it is in force, and the source it was read from -- the utility's tariff sheet (edition, sheets, authorization, file SHA-256) or an OpenEI Utility Rate Database record (its label). Charges follow the database's rate structure field for field: fixed charges per DAY or MON, energy tiers per KWH by period, time-of-use and flat demand tiers per KWT or KVA on measured demand or a contracted capacity, a reactive-demand charge per KVR, the 12 x 24 weekday and weekend schedules, the flat-demand months, the demand window and any ratchet (a fraction over months). Additions the database has no field for: dynamic pricing by a named market and region, an hours-of-use demand adjustment, the holidays the weekend schedule applies to, and riders with the date their value was read. Every rate is a currency per a UN/CEFACT Recommendation 20 unit. The gate refuses a schedule, flat-demand month or adjustment that names a period the structures do not define. Additions only.
- **New: `flow/energy-reading` 0.1.0 and `flow/grid-factor` 0.1.0.** An energy reading is one interval from a meter (MTConnect, OPC 34100, Green Button, Modbus or manual; machine, cell, facility or utility scope; WHR, KWH or MWH; metered, estimated or allocated). A grid factor is an electricity emission factor (a `ghgIntensity` per MWH or KWH) with its region, method (location-based, market-based, CBAM default or actual), combustion-only or lifecycle type, granularity, validity and the dataset and edition it is taken from; a market-based factor names its instrument or residual mix. Additions only.
- **New: `flow/custody-event` 0.1.0.** Lots becoming parts: a GS1 EPCIS 2.0 TransformationEvent in which input lots (by lot-level class or instance, with UN/CEFACT Recommendation 20 quantities) are cut, formed or joined into output parts or kits, citing the job it was recorded in (`ectropy:fabrication`). Every instance also validates against GS1's EPCIS 2.0.1 JSON Schema. Shipping and receiving, with transport mode and distance, stay material intake events.
- **`flow/logistic-unit` 0.4.0.** Additions: contents may carry their `lot`, the `originFacility` that made it (a GS1 GLN or the facility's own identifier) and their `cnCode` (EU Combined Nomenclature, 4, 6 or 8 digits). A content that names a lot must name its facility; every 0.3.0 instance stays valid.
- **`flow/carbon-report` 0.3.0.** `reportingStandard` adds `EN-15978` (the basis of the EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive's life-cycle GWP) and `CALGreen-5.409.4` beside `RICS-WLCA-2`; a line may name the `partRef` and `lotRefs` its figure covers. Additions only.
- **`_definitions/units` 1.4.0.** `ghgMass` may state its EN 15804+A2 `split` (fossil, biogenic, luluc, adding to its value) and its `lciaMethod` (IPCC-AR6, EF-3.1, TRACI-2.1). Additions only. The gate refuses a split whose parts do not add to the value.

- **`_definitions/units` 1.3.0.** Addition: C26 (millisecond, ms), a time unit of exactly 0.001 s. Every 1.2.0 instance validates unchanged.
- **New: `_definitions/configuration-ref` 0.1.0, `pm/decision` 2.3.0, `pm/inspection` 2.4.0.** Additions only: `configurationRef` names the version of the project's contract a decision or an inspection was decided under -- the contract's URN, the version's number and the SHA-256 of its canonical document -- so the record can be read against the terms it was decided under after the contract changes. Every instance valid at `pm/decision` 2.2.0 and `pm/inspection` 2.3.0 stays valid.
- **`pm/inspection` 2.3.0.** Additions only: `lane` (`standard` or `priority`; absent means standard) says how an inspection is queued, a priority inspection being raised on its own rather than waiting behind standard ones; `originRef` names the record that raised it; `status` gains `NOT_SCHEDULED`, for an inspection that is required but has no date yet. Every instance valid at 2.2.0 stays valid.
- **`voxel/voxel-group` 2.3.0.** Additions only: `rolledUpCost.priceSource`, optional, states where the members' prices came from, with the `flow/cost-rollup` price-source vocabulary (`tenant`, `reference`, `baseline`, `mixed`, `none`). Every instance valid at 2.2.0 stays valid.
- **New: `flow/cost-rollup` 0.1.0.** An estimated cost as a tree a reader can walk, from a project through its groups, voxels and elements to each assembly, zone and layer. Every node carries its `cost` (`money`, or null with an `unpricedReason`), its `quantity` and `unitCost` (`rate`), its `priceSource` (`tenant`, `reference`, `baseline`, `mixed` or `none`), its `coverage` (priced and total layers at or beneath it), its `confidence` and `asOf` date; a layer names its `product` (`productKey`) and the `priceRef` it was costed at. The rollup states its one `currency`, and optionally the `tenant` whose own prices it may use and the `region` its prices are for. Additions only.
- **One product key, and region as a jurisdiction area (breaking, draft schemas).**
  - **`flow/flow-common` 0.6.0.** New `productKey` -- a catalog product as the manufacturer's slug and the manufacturer's own product identifier -- and `jurisdictionArea`, an area named by its published scheme and code (ISO 3166, US Census and OMB, Statistics Canada). Additions only.
  - **`flow/logistic-unit` 0.3.0, `flow/intake/material-intake` 0.3.0, `flow/vendor-price-submission` 0.4.0.** `bpcProduct` is a `productKey`, not a free string; an instance carrying a string fails.
  - **`flow/vendor-price-submission` 0.4.0.** `catalogProductKey` (0.3.0) is removed: `bpcProduct` names the same product. A product may be identified by `bpcProduct` alone. `region` is a `jurisdictionArea`; absent, the price applies nationally.
- **`flow/vendor-price-submission` 0.3.0.** Additions, and one narrowing. A price may be entered by a member of the receiving tenant as well as by the vendor: `vendorRef` or the new `submittedBy` is required, and the price belongs to `targetTenant` either way. New optional `priceKind` (`vendor-published`, `vendor-invoice`, `contracted`, `estimated`), `supersedes`, `basis`, `provenance` and `sourceDocument` (the price list or quote, as a file reference). A product may be identified by `catalogProductKey`, the manufacturer's slug and its own product identifier. `priceUom` must be a code in the `_definitions/units` UN/CEFACT Recommendation 20 catalog, not any two- or three-character code; this is the one change an instance valid at 0.2.0 can fail.
- **`voxel/voxel-group` 2.2.0.** `rolledUpCost.currency` is any ISO 4217 code from `_definitions/units`, not only USD.
- **New: `_definitions/file-reference` 0.1.0.** A reference to a file a record depends on and does not embed: `uri`, `sha256`, `contentType` (the IANA media type an Asset Administration Shell File element carries), and optionally `sizeBytes`, `format` (for example `dstv-nc-8.0`, `dstv-xas`, `ifc-4.3`, `aasx`) and `provenance`. `flow/intake/bag-pointer` is unchanged.
- **New: `_definitions/classification-ref` 0.1.0.** A record's class in one published system -- `cci`, `etim`, `ifc`, `bsdd`, `iec-81346-aspect`, the Uniclass and OmniClass tables, `uniformat-ii` or `din-276` -- with its licence, whether it was verified against the publisher (a VERIFIED reference names how and against which version) and when it was observed.
- **`flow/flow-common` 0.5.0.** Additions only: a `unitId` may be an IEC 61406 identification link, an https URL under the issuer's own domain; a `partyUri` may be issued under any issuer's own domain, as a location URI already may. Every instance valid at 0.4.0 stays valid.
- **`_definitions/units` 1.2.0.** Addition: `ghgIntensity`, a greenhouse-gas mass per one unit of something (its value, unit, gas, GWP basis and the unit it is per), the form a product's declared impact takes.
- **`_definitions/units` 1.1.0.** Additions only: LTN (long ton), STC (stick) and the Recommendation 21 package codes XBG (bag), XRO (roll) and XST (sheet), each a count of packages, not of what they hold.
- **New: `_definitions/units` 1.0.0.** One definition of units, quantities, money, time and ratios for every record. `uom` is a UN/CEFACT Recommendation 20 common code (revision 11e) from a catalog that names each code's title, symbol, quantity kind, factor to the coherent SI unit (exact, or to NIST SP 811's seven significant digits) and the spellings sources use for it. `currency` is an ISO 4217 code with its minor-unit exponent (the maintenance agency's list of 2026-09-17). With them: `quantity`, `rate` (numerator per denominator), `money` (a decimal amount and its currency), `instant`, `date`, `duration` (ISO 8601), `fraction`, `ghgMass` (a greenhouse-gas mass with its gas and GWP basis) and `coordinateUnits`. Nothing existing changes in this release; the schemas move to these definitions in their own releases.
- **The line between the standard and the work on it.** The standard holds what a record is at a handoff between firms; how a record is proven, checked or decided is not part of it. Public text says what a field is, never how it is computed, and the gate holds this.
  - **New: `_definitions/provenance` 0.1.0.** A record carries proof in a provenance envelope: the proof scheme's identifier, the proof as an opaque value, and optionally when and by whom it was issued. `pm/decision`, `pm/consequence`, `pm/inspection` 2.2.0, `pm/schedule-proposal` 2.1.0 and `voxel/voxel-v3` 3.0.0 each carry an optional `provenance` list.
  - **`voxel/voxel-v3` 3.0.0 (breaking).** An acknowledgment identifies its worker by `workerRef` only: `workerName` is removed and the acknowledgment is closed.
  - **`flow/flow-common` 0.4.0.** A location URI may be issued under any issuer's own domain, not one fixed domain.
  - **Descriptions.** `voxel/voxel-group` 2.1.0, `voxel/voxel-v3` and `resource/resource-status` 2.0.1 describe their fields only. The record metadata no longer declares `syncStatus`.
- **PM records 2.1.0, voxel 2.1.0.** Additions only; every instance valid at 2.0.0 stays valid.
  - `pm/decision`: `decisionId` accepts any hyphen-separated alphanumeric identifier whose first segment starts with a letter (`D-0001` now validates, as its own description shows). `voxelRef` is optional, for decisions with no spatial location. New optional `analysis`: situation, risk if unresolved, who can decide, pre-approval, recommended action.
  - `pm/inspection`: new optional `decisionResults` (each reviewed decision and its result), `evidence`, and `result.conditions`.
  - `pm/consequence`: `category` adds `COORDINATION_CONFLICT`, `TOLERANCE_VARIANCE`, `MATERIAL_MISMATCH` and `ACCESS_ISSUE`.
  - `voxel/voxel-v3`: a tolerance override's `standardValue` and `approvedValue` carry an optional `tolerance`, the permitted deviation.
- **Pinned versions.** Every published version of every schema is served, unchanged, at `https://schemas.ectropy.ai/v/{version}/{path}`. `index.json` lists each schema's versions and maturity.
- **Immutable versions.** The gate fails when a schema's content changes without a version bump.
- **Maturity.** Below 1.0.0 a schema is draft; from 1.0.0 it is stable.
- The site no longer advertises an npm package.
- This repository no longer publishes to npm. `@ectropy/schemas` is the Universal Decision Log (0.4.x lineage); the 1.0.0 published from here on 2026-10-11 was an error and is deprecated.

## 1.0.0

The standard moves to its own home, https://schemas.ectropy.ai/, and this repository becomes the only source of the schemas, the site and the package.

**Breaking**

- **Identifiers.** Every `$id` and `$ref` moved to `https://schemas.ectropy.ai/<path>`. Each schema's version is bumped: major from 1.0.0 up, minor below.
- **One URN definition.** The URN is defined once, at `_definitions/urn.schema.json`, and every schema references it. The `pm/*` schemas had their own narrower URN pattern. They now accept the shared form: `{id}` may carry further colon-separated segments.
- **No `const` on `$schema`.** The `pm/*` schemas no longer require `$schema` to be one fixed string. It is a plain URI, as in every other schema.

**Changed**

- `resource-pose` references `resource-status` for `resourceType`, `availability` and `activity`, and `voxel-group` for `point3`, instead of carrying copies. Validation is unchanged.
- Non-validating metadata (`definitions._metadata`, `x-authoring`) removed. Descriptions describe the shape and its fields only.
- The JSON-LD extension namespace used in intake examples is `https://schemas.ectropy.ai/flow-extension#`.
